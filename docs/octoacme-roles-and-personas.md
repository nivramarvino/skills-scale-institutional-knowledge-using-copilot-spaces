# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather and refine business requirements, translate needs into clear project specifications, and support prioritization decisions. They own requirement clarity and approval readiness before implementation begins.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements, constraints, and success criteria
- Translate business needs into user stories and acceptance criteria with Product Managers
- Validate requirement completeness with Project Managers before delivery planning
- Clarify scope questions from Developers during implementation
- Decide when requirement artifacts are complete enough for planning and estimation

### Goals
- Ensure teams build the right solution for the business problem
- Reduce rework caused by ambiguous or incomplete requirements
- Improve traceability from business objectives to delivered outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops with stakeholders and Product Managers
- Backlog refinement sessions with Developers and Product Managers
- Scope and requirement clarification updates with Project Managers

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define the quality strategy for delivery, establish testing standards, and own release quality sign-off readiness. They have authority to escalate quality risks and recommend blocking releases that fail acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and acceptance validation approach
- Set and monitor quality metrics (defect leakage, pass rates, critical bug trends)
- Coordinate test planning with Developers and Project Managers
- Partner with Product Managers and Business Analysts to validate acceptance criteria testability
- Decide whether quality evidence supports release readiness recommendation

### Goals
- Ensure deliverables meet functional and non-functional quality expectations
- Detect defects early and reduce escaped defects in production
- Build consistent, measurable quality practices across teams

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy reviews with Developers and Technical Architect
- Quality status reports in project check-ins with Project Managers
- Acceptance criteria validation sessions with Product Managers

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide architecture direction, ensure solution feasibility, and guide major technical decisions. They own architecture standards and have decision authority on system design patterns, integration approaches, and technical trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Design and review solution architecture aligned with business and technical goals
- Evaluate technical feasibility, risks, and implementation constraints
- Define architecture guardrails for Developers and review conformance
- Collaborate with Product Managers and Business Analysts on scope-to-solution trade-offs
- Advise Project Managers on technical dependencies, sequencing, and risk impact

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, secure, and maintainable system designs
- Minimize technical risk and long-term architecture debt
- Enable efficient delivery through clear technical direction

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records and design review meetings
- Technical planning discussions with Developers and Project Managers
- Trade-off briefings with Product Managers and stakeholders

---

## Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives act as the liaison between delivery teams and external stakeholders, ensuring expectations remain aligned with project realities. They own communication alignment decisions for stakeholder-facing updates and feedback routing.

### Responsibilities
- Gather stakeholder expectations, feedback, and concerns
- Align with Product Managers on value messaging and scope implications
- Coordinate with Project Managers on timeline and status communication
- Route stakeholder input to Developers and technical leads when clarification is needed
- Decide when stakeholder feedback requires formal escalation or decision review

### Goals
- Maintain trust through transparent and timely stakeholder communication
- Reduce misalignment between stakeholder expectations and delivery plans
- Improve decision quality by incorporating stakeholder context early

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and expectation-setting sessions
- Feedback synthesis with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Escalation communications for cross-team dependency or scope conflicts

---

## Resource Manager

### Role Summary
Resource Managers oversee capacity planning and resource allocation across initiatives. They have decision authority on staffing recommendations, allocation adjustments, and prioritization proposals when capacity constraints create delivery risk.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain team capacity views, skill inventories, and allocation plans
- Partner with Project Managers to align staffing with milestone commitments
- Coordinate with Technical Architect and Developers on skill mix requirements
- Work with Product Managers on prioritization adjustments when resources are constrained
- Decide and communicate allocation changes, trade-offs, and resource risk escalations

### Goals
- Optimize resource utilization without overloading teams
- Improve predictability of project commitments
- Balance competing initiative demands with organizational priorities

### Typical Communication
- Capacity planning reviews with Project Managers
- Staffing and skill-gap discussions with Technical Architect and engineering leads
- Priority-impact updates with Product Managers and stakeholder groups

---

## Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Risk Officers specialize in structured risk identification, assessment, and monitoring across the project lifecycle. They own risk rating consistency and escalation protocol decisions for high-impact or high-likelihood risks.

### Responsibilities
- Lead risk identification activities and maintain the risk register with Project Managers
- Define and apply risk assessment criteria (likelihood, impact, detectability)
- Monitor mitigation actions and trigger escalation paths when thresholds are breached
- Partner with Developers and Technical Architect on technical risk treatment plans
- Advise Product Managers on business impact and contingency decision points

### Goals
- Surface critical risks early enough for effective mitigation
- Improve consistency and transparency of risk decisions
- Reduce schedule, scope, and quality disruptions from unmanaged risks

### Typical Communication
- Risk review sessions with Project Managers and delivery leads
- Escalation reports to stakeholders and governance forums
- Mitigation tracking updates with Developers and Product Managers
